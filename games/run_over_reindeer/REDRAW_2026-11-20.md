# Run Over Reindeer: math 2026.11.20, minimums redrawn (STOPPED at the shaping choice)

Corey 2026-11-20: "They have to reach these minimums naturally, and never tell the player", then
"Yes redraw the math and change the rules, this should be invisible to the player".

**Status:** the engine change is made, the production books are built and pass every book gate.
The lookup tables are NOT shaped: the 2026.11.18 shaper cannot reach 96.7% in `ante_10` on the
redrawn books, and in every base-type mode the redraw forces a choice between a much rarer bonus
(about 23% fewer triggers) and natural rounds tilted toward their smaller wins. Per the brief, this
report sets out the options with numbers and chooses none. Nothing game-side was done. Nothing was
uploaded or copied to staging.

## 1. What changed in the engine

- `ror_engine.Engine.round`: after the trigger spin, the bonus round is played; if its OWN free
  spins (retriggered spins included, the trigger spin's win not counted, exactly what the old
  `featureWin` measured) total less than its minimum, the round is thrown away and played again
  from the same trigger spin, until it reaches the minimum. The record (what the book is written
  from) keeps only the kept round. The minimum is bounded by the room left under 7,777x, so a
  trigger spin near the cap cannot loop. A retrigger only adds spins inside the round, and there
  is no upgrade path, so every round ends at this one check. Both top-up code paths are deleted.
- Minimums unchanged (`R.ROUND_MINIMUM`, set by `variant_f.apply`): natural Free Spins 10x, natural
  Super 50x, bought Super 20x, bought Free Spins none. `R.TOPUP` / `R.TOPUP_BOUGHT` are cleared and
  unread.
- `gamestate_f.py` and `gamestate.py`: the `floorTopUp` writer is gone (an assert guards it).
  `game_events_f.py`: no `FLOOR_TOP_UP`.
- `MATH_VERSION` 2026.11.20; contract **2026-11-20a** (2026-11-15a minus `floorTopUp`;
  `EVENT_SCHEMA.md` top section). No other event or field changed.
- The win cap and `wincap` behave as before.
- Pre-change copies: `results/build_2026-11-20/pre_change/`.

## 2. Why the shaper cannot simply absorb it

The top-ups were worth about 1 RTP point (0.68 natural Free Spins, 0.31 natural Super). The redraw
is much bigger than that. It does not lift a short round to 10x; it replaces it with a whole new
round. About 1 natural round in 4 was short (24.0% of Free Spins at a mean of 3.9x, 24.6% of Supers
at 22.8x), and each is now replaced by an average round of about 130x / 380x. Under the 2026.11.18
weight rules (the same trigger rates and tilts), the redrawn books pay:

| mode | base | ante_2 | ante_4 | ante_10 | bonus | super |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| RTP before reshaping | 114.91% | 120.40% | 122.62% | 123.59% | 96.70% (unchanged books) | 108.88% |

The natural round means rise from 106.2x / 316.8x (base) and 101.1x / 296.0x (antes) to
137.2x / 410.6x and 130.5x / 383x. With the shaper's own rule (natural mean = the deck's mean,
x LIFT in base), only the trigger rate can absorb that. In `ante_10`, Super is fixed at 1 in 40;
at 381x a round that alone is 9.5x of the 9.67x budget, so no Free Spins rate works. Even with no
Free Spins at all, Super could be at most 1 in 40.6.

The bought modes have only one route (96.7% of the cost): the Free Spins buy is unchanged (no
minimum, byte-identical books). The bought Super deck now averages about 327x, so the existing
tilt trims it to 290.1x (tilt a -0.0006 -> +0.084): its max win goes 1 in 2,906 -> 1 in 3,387 and
SD 517.8 -> 489.5. That part is the same in every option.

## 3. The options (every one at 96.7000% in every mode; nothing forbidden is touched)

- **A, the shaper as it stands:** natural rounds keep their natural mix (no extra tilt); the
  triggers get rarer. Base Free Spins 1 in 212.8 -> **275.2**, Super 2,127.6 -> **2,751.5**;
  ante_2 Free Spins 76.4 -> 105.8; ante_4 42.6 -> 64.4. **`ante_10` has no solution.**
- **A\*, A with `ante_10` fixed by moving its Super rate:** Free Spins stays 1 in 51.4, Super
  1 in 40 -> **1 in 55.8** (Corey's published "1 in 40" changes).
- **B, keep every published trigger rate:** the natural rounds are tilted down to their 2026.11.18
  means (tilt a about +0.13 to +0.22, from about -0.03). Every feature-menu number stays. The cost:
  big natural wins get rarer (base max win 1 in 2.97M -> **4.42M**, ante_2 1.44M -> 2.20M, ante_4
  504k -> 789k, ante_10 113k -> 182k), SD 24.7 -> 22.5, and slightly more of the natural rounds sit
  in the 10x-20x band (22.4% against A's 18.3%, still smooth, no spike).
- **C, halfway:** base Free Spins 1 in 244, Super 1 in 2,440; ante_2 1 in 90.6; ante_4 1 in 52.7;
  ante_10 keeps Super 1 in 40 but Free Spins falls to **1 in 128** (from 51.4), since Super takes
  most of that budget.

Other mixes are possible (for example B in the antes and A in base); each is a shaper setting,
about 5 minutes to apply, with the books as they are.

## 5. Wins just above each minimum (is there a pile-up?)

Share of that kind of round, by its own win. The full tables (every 0.1x step near the minimum,
ten bands to twice the minimum, every option) are in `results/build_2026-11-20/tables.md`.

| | 2026.11.18 | A | B | C | reference |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| natural Free Spins at exactly 10.0x | **23.98%** | 0.22% | 0.28% | 0.25% | bought Free Spins at 10.0x: 0.18% |
| natural Free Spins 10.0x-20x (incl. 10.0) | 37.9% | 18.3% | 22.4% | 20.2% | |
| ... each 1x band from 10 to 20 | 1.1-1.7% | 1.5-2.2% | 1.7-2.8% | 1.6-2.5% | bought: 1.2-1.7% |
| natural Super at exactly 50.0x | **24.62%** | 0.16% | 0.22% | 0.19% | |
| natural Super 50-100x | 43.2% | 24.5% | 30.7% | 27.3% | |
| bought Super at exactly 20.0x | **12.12%** | 0.15% | 0.15% | 0.15% | |
| bought Super 20-40x | 21.3% | 12.1% | 12.1% | 12.1% | |

**Plainly:** the 2026.11.18 math HAD a pile-up: a quarter of natural rounds ended at exactly the
minimum. In the redrawn books there is none in any option. The share at exactly 10.0x / 50.0x /
20.0x is the same as at the 0.1x steps around it, and the bands from the minimum to twice it fall
off smoothly, like the bought Free Spins (no minimum) over the same range. What a player can notice
is the edge itself: a natural round never pays its own spins under 10x (50x Super). That is the
rule Corey chose. Option B tilts more weight toward the low end (about a fifth more per band than
A), still smooth.
